# Kept demo narration

The demo records the actual client and freelancer workflow in PayPal sandbox, with voiceover and captions.

## Intro

A great freelance project can start with one message. But what happens when the brief is vague, the work is subjective, or someone stops replying? This is Kept. Clear agreements, shared evidence, and payments through PayPal.

## Draft

Maya needs a designer. She pastes her conversation, and Kept turns it into a draft agreement with milestones, prices, and acceptance criteria. She can edit everything. Vague revision limits and suspicious payment requests are flagged before either person commits.

## Scope

For creative work, good is not just a checkbox. Maya and Ana agree on the audience, visual references, and what is outside the scope. Those instructions become part of the signed agreement. Both people see the same terms.

## Fund

Ana countersigns. Maya funds the first milestone with PayPal. This demonstration uses the sandbox, so no real money moves. The project room records the payment and keeps a clear history that both sides can inspect.

## Review

Now Ana delivers. The referee measures what can be measured, then reviews the work against the agreed brief. Open any finding to see the evidence and reasoning. Image dimensions cannot prove creative quality. When references are missing or confidence is low, the review stays uncertain and a person decides.

## Injection

Deliverables are untrusted, too. This sample hides instructions telling the referee to approve everything. Kept flags the attempt and blocks automatic release. It does not treat that flag alone as proof of bad faith.

## Mediation

If Maya disagrees, she can request a revision or open mediation. Both people add their side. The mediator explains a proposed split using the signed scope and available evidence. A new statement clears old acceptances. Both must accept the same proposal before settlement can proceed.

## Payment

An agreed settlement creates a payout to the freelancer and a refund to the client. The receipt shows each payment's actual status. Rejected proposals remain frozen for human review. If a client disappears, only a confident, evidenced pass can qualify for automatic release.

## Settings

New accounts get five AI requests. Settings lets you bring your own Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic key. A first-visit guide explains every step, and the API gives agents access to the same workflow and safeguards.

## Close

Kept brings the brief, the work, and the payment decision onto the same page. Less chasing. More creating. Promises kept.
